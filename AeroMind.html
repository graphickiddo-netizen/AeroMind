Five dimensions of pilot readiness

Each domain draws from a bank of 4 questions — 2 are randomly selected per session. This creates a unique, unpredictable questionnaire every time.

🧠
Cognitive Readiness
Measures mental sharpness, concentration, decision-making speed, and procedural recall. Critical for split-second in-flight judgments.
30% FRI Weight
💜
Emotional Stability
Evaluates stress management, emotional balance, separation of personal concerns from duty, and overall psychological composure.
20% FRI Weight
💪
Physical Condition
Assesses rest quality, physical energy, freedom from pain or illness, hydration/nutrition status, and substance clearance before duty.
25% FRI Weight
🎯
Situational Awareness
Gauges environmental awareness, risk assessment confidence, NOTAM/weather currency, and preparedness for unexpected scenarios.
15% FRI Weight
👥
Social / CRM
Measures crew communication comfort, crew alignment, CRM effectiveness, and confidence in mutual support during operations.
10% FRI Weight
🍺
Substance & Medication
Mandatory screening for alcohol consumption and medication usage. Direct regulatory violations are flagged per ICAO Annex 1 and FAA 91.17.
Auto Disqualifier

// Assessment Protocol

How the FRI is calculated

Six stages — from secure login to GO/NO-GO decision — completed in under 5 minutes.

🔐
Step 01
Secure Authentication
Pilots register with a verified email address and organisation details. Credentials are stored locally and used to personalise each session.
📋
Step 02
Pilot Profile Collection
Name, rank, flight ID, hours slept, and hours on duty are recorded. Sleep and duty data directly influence the Fatigue Index penalty.
🎲
Step 03
Random 10-Question Draw
2 questions per domain are selected randomly from a pool of 4 each — plus a mandatory substance screen. No two sessions are identical.
⚙️
Step 04
Weighted Scoring Engine
Domain averages combine using evidence-based weights: Cognitive 30%, Physical 25%, Emotional 20%, Situational 15%, Social 10%.
⚡
Step 05
Fatigue & Substance Penalty
Sleep deficit, excessive duty hours, alcohol use, and impairing medication trigger automatic FRI penalties. Some conditions result in immediate grounding.
✅
Step 06
GO / NO-GO + Regulatory Report
The FRI maps to one of five statuses. For scores below threshold: historical crash correlations, ICAO/FAA violations, and randomised root-cause reasons are generated.
